# Clôd Cluster Discord Server Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Clôd Cluster ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our Discord server ("Server").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

We may collect information you provide directly when you:
- Register or join our Server
- Communicate with other users or administrators
- Participate in surveys, feedback sessions, or other activities
- Submit support requests or questions

This information may include:
- Your Discord username and discriminator
- Profile information visible on Discord
- Content of messages and communications
- Feedback and survey responses

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Server, we and third-party services (including Discord) may automatically collect certain information, including:
- Usage data (such as when you access the Server and how you interact with it)
- Device information (such as IP address and browser type)
- Log data (such as error reports and activity patterns)

### 1.3 Bot Data Collection

If we use bots on our Server, they may collect additional information such as:
- Command usage
- Message content when interacting with the bot
- User activity patterns for bot functionality

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Operate and maintain the Server
- Improve user experience
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Communicate with users
- Develop new features and services
- Protect the security and integrity of the Server
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information in the following circumstances:

### 3.1 With Your Consent
We may share information when you give us specific consent to do so.

### 3.2 Service Providers
We may share information with third-party vendors, consultants, and other service providers who need access to such information to perform work on our behalf.

### 3.3 Compliance with Laws
We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

### 3.4 Protection of Rights
We may disclose information when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

## 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:
- Access to your personal information
- Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
- Deletion of your personal information
- Restriction or objection to processing
- Data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided in the "Contact Us" section.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our Server is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than the country in which you reside. These countries may have data protection laws that are different from the laws of your country.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on the Server. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## 10. Discord's Privacy Policy

As our Server operates on Discord, your information is also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review Discord's Privacy Policy at https://discord.com/privacy.

## 11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [claude@clod.app](mailto:claude@clod.app).
